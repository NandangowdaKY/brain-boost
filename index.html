Section 01 / Introduction to BrainBoost
Biology × Computer Science Mini-Project

Train Your Brain. Sharpen Your Memory.

Your brain works like a muscle. Every time you learn, focus, or remember something, tiny nerve cells called neurons send electrical and chemical signals to each other. The more you practice recalling information, the stronger these pathways become. BrainBoost combines Biology and Computer Science to explain how memory works in simple terms and gives you 7 quick daily exercises to test and improve your focus, recall, and reaction speed.

01 / Neural Signals

How Neurons Talk

Neurons pass tiny electrical sparks and chemical messages across small gaps called synapses to store what you see, read, and learn every day.

02 / 3-Step Process

How Memory Forms

Memory happens in three simple steps—paying close attention (Encoding), saving it inside the brain (Storage), and bringing it back when needed (Recall).

03 / Daily Practice

Why Brain Games Help

Challenging your brain with numbers, patterns, words, and speed exercises keeps your neural pathways active and sharpens daily concentration.

Section 04 / Project & Contributors

Team Information & Project Details

About the Mini-Project

BrainBoost: Brain Health and Memory Training Website is an interdisciplinary mini-project connecting Biology (Neuroscience) with Computer Science (Web Development).

The platform explains how neurons communicate and how memories are formed in clear, accessible language while providing 7 interactive browser exercises built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript to train working memory, spatial recall, verbal recognition, selective attention, and reaction speed.
